DermAssist - Demo Guide for Hackathon 🏆
=========================================

----------------------------
"DermAssist is a privacy-first mobile app that uses Edge AI to perform 
instant skin condition analysis - completely offline. No cloud, no uploads, 
results in under 1 second. Your medical data never leaves your device."

-------------------

------------------------

1. APP LAUNCH (10 seconds)
   - Show splash screen animation

   - Point to "AI Model Ready" status

3. CAMERA SCREEN (20 seconds)

   - Highlight speed: "Watch how fast this is"
   - Point out: "All happening on your device"

   - Risk Level Badge: Color-coded (Red/Orange/Green)
   - Confidence Meter: Animated progress bar
   - Performance Metrics:
     * Processing time: ~100-500ms
     * On-Device: 100%
   - Medical Disclaimer: Professional touch
   
   KEY TALKING POINTS:

6. SCAN AGAIN (10 seconds)
   - Tap "Scan Again" button
   - Show smooth navigation back
   - Demonstrate multiple scans

TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS FOR JUDGES
--------------------------------

Architecture:
- Flutter 3.x (Cross-platform)
- TensorFlow Lite (Edge AI)
- MobileNetV2 (Optimized CNN)
- INT8 Quantization (4x compression)
- Provider State Management

ML Pipeline:
- Input: 224x224 RGB
- Preprocessing: Decode → Resize → Normalize
- Inference: <500ms on mid-range device
- Output: 7-class probability distribution
- Post-processing: Risk level assessment

Privacy Features:
- Zero network calls
- No cloud storage
- No data collection
- No analytics tracking
- HIPAA-compliant architecture

Performance:
- Model Size: 3.4 MB
- Cold Start: <2 seconds
- Inference: 100-500ms
- Memory: <50 MB
- Battery: Minimal impact

JUDGE QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
--------------------------

Q: "How accurate is the model?"
A: "We're using MobileNetV2 trained on dermatology datasets. 
    For production, we'd fine-tune on HAM10000 (10,000 images) 
    and ISIC datasets for medical-grade accuracy."

Q: "Why not use cloud AI?"
A: "Privacy is paramount in healthcare. Edge AI means:
    - No HIPAA compliance issues
    - Works offline (rural areas, no signal)
    - Instant results (no network latency)
    - Zero data breach risk"

Q: "Can this scale?"
A: "Absolutely! Edge AI scales perfectly:
    - No server costs
    - No bandwidth costs
    - Works on any device
    - Millions of users = zero infrastructure"

Q: "What about false positives?"
A: "We show confidence levels and risk assessment.
    High-risk conditions get flagged for immediate 
    medical attention. We're a screening tool, not 
    a replacement for dermatologists."

Q: "Future roadmap?"
A: "1. 3D visualization with heatmaps
     2. Skin diary (track changes over time)
     3. Multi-language support
     4. Educational content per condition
     5. Integration with telemedicine platforms"
